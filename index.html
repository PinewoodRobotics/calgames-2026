What is Calgames?

Calgames is an offseason tournament that, using the previous season's game, gives teams an opportunity to train members in a competition environment before the season starts in January.

It is one of the few tournaments we attend this close to Pinewood; if you're able to visit to support the team, we'd greatly appreciate it!
