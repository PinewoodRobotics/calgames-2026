What is Calgames?

Calgames is an offseason tournament that, using the previous season's game, gives teams an opportunity to train members in a competition environment before the season starts in January.

It is one of the few tournaments we attend this close to Pinewood; if you're able to visit to support the team, we'd greatly appreciate it!

When Should I Attend?

Qualification Matches—join us in the stands to see a variety of robots competing in back-to-back matches. Our exact match schedule is posted closer to the start of the tournament; we'll update this page when that information is released.

Alliance Selection—when qualifications conclude, the top eight teams choose their alliance partners for the playoffs. It's a suspensful moment of excitement and anticipation for teams and the audience alike.

Playoffs—while we can't guarantee we'll be selected, the finals are an exciting series of matches where top robot alliances compete back-to-back in a double elimination bracket.

Lunch? Maybe. While we don't know for certain, touraments often have food available for purchase. Teams typically order their own food, but we can't guarantee we'll have enough for all visitors.

Key Locations

Arena—the game field. You may find some of us in the stands; search for neon green, the sequence 4765, or our distinctive powerup logo.

Pits—robot repair and maintenance area. Each team gets a 10x10x10 foot cube to set up a workshop and service their robot between matches. It's what goes on here that makes each match possible. Feel free to visit, just be aware this is an active area and saftey glasses are required.
